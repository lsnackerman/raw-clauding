# Alive

*Operational state: what's pending, by area. Sections sit in priority order and on-deck items sit at the top of each, so `/pickup` reads this top-down: follow-ups first, then each area. Every entry is a name plus a pointer to where the detail lives, never the reasoning. When something's done it leaves the list, no checkmark. Thin is normal, especially early. Direction and goals → `VISION.md`. Ideas → `SCRATCHPAD.md`.*

---

## FOLLOW-UPS (owed)
*What's live between you and other people, across every area: who you owe something, who you're waiting on. One line each, pointing at the person. It gets its own section because that question spans all the areas below and you ask it as a whole. Cross something off by deleting it.*

---

## NEXT - {AREA}
*One `## NEXT - {AREA}` section per area of your life (work, personal, learning, a side project, whatever shape it takes). Areas emerge as you use the system; a new one earns its heading when the first item that belongs in it arrives. On-deck items at the top.*
