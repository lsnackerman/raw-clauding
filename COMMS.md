# Comms

*Your comms draft zone. Messages in flight (emails, DMs, posts) before they're sent and before they're filed.*

---

Comms get their own catch-all, separate from SCRATCHPAD, because a message in flight has a different shape than a loose idea: it's addressed to someone, it gets drafted and redrafted, and once it's sent it wants to live with the person or audience it went to.

Drop drafts here while you're working them. When a thread settles, route it by who it was for:

- **One-to-one** (a specific person) → with that person in your `Library/` (make them a home, e.g. a `people/` folder, as you go)
- **One-to-many** (a list, a channel, an audience) → with your outgoing/broadcast comms in `Projects/`

**How to use it**:

- Draft outgoing messages here; paste in replies you want to sit with before answering
- Keep the back-and-forth together while a thread is live; the version trail is useful
- Mark what's a draft vs. what's sent (a quick `— DRAFT, not sent` saves confusion later)
- Route during `/wrap` (or whenever); the AI will help move settled threads to the right home and leave the live ones here
- Once a thread is filed with its person or audience, clear it from here

**Why it earns its place at root**: comms are where your thinking meets other people, so they deserve a low-friction catch-all of their own. Keeping drafts out of SCRATCHPAD (loose ideas) keeps each catch-all scannable, and routing by recipient means past comms live *with* the relationship, so next time you write to someone, the history is right there.
