# Scratchpad

*Your brain dump zone. System-wide catch-all for notes, ideas, rough thoughts, half-formed riffs.*

---

Get it out of your head and onto the page. Don't worry about organizing. That's not this file's job. The point is to capture before things evaporate, then route when you have energy (or let the AI route during `/wrap`).

**How to use it**:

- Drop ideas as they come: full sentences, fragments, links, screenshots, whatever shape the thought arrives in
- Don't worry about hierarchy or where things "should" live
- Route during `/wrap` (or whenever); the AI will help find better homes for things that earned them
- After routing, prune what's been captured elsewhere

**Why it earns its place at root**: capture is foundational to thinking with AI. Having the catch-all one keystroke away means the friction of capture stays low. The friction of routing stays at /wrap, where it belongs.
