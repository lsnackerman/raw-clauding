# You — who you are

Your space. The sister to `Core/opus/` (the AI's space). Both sit here in Core/ because both partners are participants, not subtopics.

This is where the AI learns who you are: how you think, how you like to work, the context that makes you *you* rather than "the user." Origin stories, your wiring, what energizes vs. drains you, the things a new instance would otherwise have to re-learn every session. The more the AI knows you, the more it can meet you instead of serving you.

**Rename this folder to your name if you like.** That's how the convention started (one person's system has a folder named for them). `you/` is just the starter default.

**What might live here** (discovered, not designed; write them as they show up):
- *How your brain works* — patterns you fall into, your energy rhythm, what focus feels like
- *What you're reaching toward* — curiosities, what you're building, what you're avoiding
- *How you receive things* — what lands, what doesn't, what register feels honest
- *Origin stories* — the context behind why you work the way you do

Write here as things surface. Most of it accretes through use. A session names something true about how you work, it lands here. The shape figures itself out.

The auto-loading rule `Core/rules/you.md` keeps the AI pointed at this folder, so what lands here actually gets used when it frames and proposes.
