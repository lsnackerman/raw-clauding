# Signal tuning

How to cultivate curiosity and sharpen what resonates with you.

---

## The pattern

1. **Save the source** — capture the content to your library (URL, event page, PDF, whatever form it lives in)
2. **Attend** — show up to talks, classes, content that might be relevant
3. **Capture by hand** — take notes + screenshots of things that resonate most (not everything, just what your brain flags)
4. **Process with the AI** — run /tune with your notes alongside the source material. Surface insights, route them where they belong. The conversation about what resonated IS the work.

**Why hand-selection matters:**
The act of choosing what to screenshot IS the signal. You're not transcribing - you're filtering through your curiosity. That curation is the valuable part. AI handles grunt work without losing what made you reach for the capture button.

**Key principles:**
- Don't optimize for completeness - optimize for resonance
- Your attention is the filter, not comprehensive coverage
- Integration should preserve your POV, not flatten it
- Notes stay raw - your voice, your curation. Add headings-as-signposts if helpful, don't restructure.
