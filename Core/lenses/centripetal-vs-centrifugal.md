# Centripetal vs centrifugal

*Same building blocks. Opposite directions.*

---

## The observation

The AI tools ecosystem is converging on the same primitives: skills, sessions, memory, cron jobs, sub-agents. But the same building blocks serve two opposite intentions.

**Centrifugal** (push work away from you): AI as senior employee. Chief of staff. Delegate everything. The human routes tasks to agents and reviews output. Success = you touched as little as possible.

**Centripetal** (pull thinking closer to you): AI as thinking partner. Skills aren't automation, they're crystallized thinking patterns. Sessions aren't memory for the AI, they're thinking archives for both of you. Success = you thought more deeply than you could alone.

Same vocabulary, inverted assumptions about what humans are for.

---

## Where it gets sharp

If you only use pre-made skills to delegate tasks:
- The AI does the thinking about *how*
- Other people did the thinking about *what* (they wrote the skills)
- You're just the click between them

That's not partnership. It's not even delegation. It's routing. You become the middleware. That's the exact problem these tools claim to solve.

Skills are thinking. If you never build your own, you're outsourcing both the AI thinking AND the human thinking. The system runs but nobody's home.

---

## The uncanny valley

The vocabulary overlap creates dissonance. "Skills" in a delegation system = recipes for the AI to execute without you. "Skills" in raw-clauding = recipes for how you and the AI think together. Same word, different theory of mind.

You can feel it when someone describes their system: are they getting closer to their thinking, or further from it? The answer tells you which direction they're building.

---

## At the architecture layer

The axis also runs at the level of *where the partnership sits in the loop structure*, not just what a tool pulls you toward. In a centripetal architecture the partnership is the **innermost** loop; automation lives in the loops around it and serves it. A centrifugal architecture inverts that: the agents are the core loop and humans handle exceptions, which means **the machine decides what counts as an exception**, and that seam fails silently. **A partnership you escalate *to* is a partnership the automation can gate.**

Note the trap this dissolves: automation is **not** the far end of this axis. Intention-dense automation is normal and good: a pipeline you built, hooks that carry the mechanical so the thinking stays in the loop. The variable is whether anyone's home, not whether a loop is automated. (`Core/foundations/intention-flies-the-craft.md` is the ground under this: intention is what flies the craft, and the degenerative pole is when a constraint flies it and gets called intent.)

---

## Origin

Observed during a demo of a centrifugal system (15+ channels of sub-agents handling video, UX, PR, content). The presenter's own friction: "one of the difficulties is which channel to go for for what." The human as router, experiencing the exact middleware problem the system was supposed to solve.

---

*The question isn't what tools you use. It's which direction they pull you.*
