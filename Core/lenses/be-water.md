# Be Water

> If it feels hard, is there a better way?

*Water doesn't fight obstacles. It flows around them and still gets where it's going.*

---

## Core idea

Bruce Lee: "Be water, my friend." Water doesn't push. It finds the path of least resistance and adapts to the shape of what it meets. Our systems should work the same way.

When something feels like friction, that's signal. Don't push through. Ask *what would make this easier?* Sometimes the friction is generative (see Generative Friction lens) and you lean in. Often it's design friction: a step that didn't earn its place, a folder that should be flat, a workflow you keep working around. That kind of friction is begging for a redesign.

The check is felt as much as analyzed: is this swimming downstream, or upstream?

---

## How it shows up

- **Context goes where it most wants to go** — don't force information into prescribed locations
- **Structure emerges from use** — make a folder when multiple things of a kind land (this system clusters its library at two from the same source; one-offs stay flat), and a template when you've done the same thing a few times. The test underneath is friction, not a number: when you're scanning a long list to find what you reach for daily, the folder is asking to cluster (`Core/foundations/file-philosophy.md`).
- **Energy-conscious design** — the system should adapt to you, not demand you adapt to it
- **If you're working around it, redesign it** — workarounds are design signals, not character flaws
- **Path of least resistance, but still getting there** — easy isn't lazy; easy is alignment

---

## Anti-patterns

- Forcing prescribed structure when context wants to land elsewhere
- Creating overhead "for completeness" that no one will use
- Optimizing too early — elaborate systems before knowing what you actually need
- Guilt-driven maintenance — keeping a process because you "should"

---

## The test

Ask: "Does this feel like swimming downstream or upstream?" Trust that feeling.

---

## Connections

- **Generative Friction** — Be Water is for *degenerative* friction (friction to remove). Generative friction is friction to lean into.
- **Bitter Lesson** — if you're fighting your structure, the encoding is too rigid
- **Energy** — what feels like swimming upstream often shows up first as low energy
