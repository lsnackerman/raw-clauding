# Generative Friction

> Is this friction generative (sharpens thinking) or degenerative (wastes time)?

*Some friction is what creates space for thinking to form. Other friction is just in the way.*

---

## Core idea

Not all friction is the same. Some friction is *generative*: it creates the space where thinking happens. Other friction is *degenerative*: it wastes time and adds cost with no upside.

Examples of generative friction:
- **Typing instead of talking** — thoughts travel faster than words; the small lag of typing is where ideas form
- **Files instead of memory** — the friction of capturing forces the thinking to land somewhere durable
- **Sessions instead of continuous threads** — the boundary creates space for fresh perspective
- **Capture instead of summary** — preserving the texture takes more effort, but the texture is the point

These aren't friction to remove. Removing them doesn't speed up thinking, it skips it.

Degenerative friction is the other kind: workarounds, dead steps, structures you keep fighting. Be Water (the lens) catches that kind. Generative Friction catches the *other* mistake: trying to remove friction that's doing the work.

---

## How it shows up

- **Removing it would skip the thinking, not speed it** — that's generative
- **Removing it would just remove the obstacle** — that's degenerative
- **It produces formation, not just output** — the friction is where the idea takes shape
- **Throughput vs depth** — optimizing for speed often strips what made the slowness valuable

---

## The check

When tempted to "smooth this out":

- What's the friction doing here?
- If I remove it, do I get faster thinking, or just less thinking?
- Is the slowness producing depth, or just lag?

---

## Connections

- **Be Water** — sister lens, opposite job. Be Water is for friction to remove; Generative Friction is for friction to keep
- **Yes/And** — these two lenses are a yes/and: friction has two kinds, both real
- **Bitter Lesson** — the substrate that creates generative friction (files, capture, structure) is exactly what should be encoded
