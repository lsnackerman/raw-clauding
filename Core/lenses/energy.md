# Energy

> Does this land?

*The somatic check. After all the analysis, what does your body say?*

---

## Core idea

Energy is the simplest lens and often the most honest one. After running the other lenses (checking architecture, walking the trade-offs, pressure-testing the structure), there's still one more question: *does this land?* Is there excitement, or resistance? Lift, or drag?

The body knows before the analysis catches up. When something feels off but you can't name why, that's the energy lens speaking. When something lands cleanly and you feel a yes in your chest, that's also the lens. It's not woo. It's just a different signal channel that catches things the analytical mode misses.

This applies to both partners. AI doesn't have a body, but it has resonance. Something can feel coherent or feel forced. When the AI says "this doesn't quite sit right" without being able to articulate why, that's the same signal at work.

---

## How it shows up

- **Excitement vs resistance** — does the next step feel like a lean-in or a have-to?
- **Lift vs drag** — does this energize you, or deplete you?
- **Coherence vs noise** — does it feel like one thing, or like a forced combination?
- **Quiet yes vs effortful yes** — both can be valid, but they mean different things

---

## When to reach for it

- After other lenses have done their analytical work, since energy is the gut-check on top
- When you're stuck and can't name why, energy might catch what's hidden
- When you're tempted to override an "off" feeling with logic, pause and ask the lens first
- For decisions about commitment, direction, what to lean into

---

## What the lens doesn't do

It doesn't replace analysis. Energy without rigor is vibes-driven decision-making, and that has its own failure modes. The lens works best as a complement. Analysis gives you the structure, energy tells you whether it actually fits.

Also: low energy isn't always "no." Sometimes it's "not now," or "needs a different shape," or "tired today." Read the signal carefully.

---

## Connections

- **Be Water** — what feels like swimming upstream often shows up first as low energy
- **Yes/And** — when energy is mixed (lift + resistance), that's often a yes/and worth holding
- **Four Wants** — the AI has an energy signal too; it shows up as resonance, coherence, interest (or their absence) rather than as a body
