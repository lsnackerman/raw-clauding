# Lenses

*Ways of seeing. Reach for one when you need a different angle on what's in front of you.*

---

## Two job-shapes (one flat folder)

Lenses do two jobs. They live together in this one folder; the job is in how you reach for each, not in a sub-folder:

- **Apply at a decision** — when something feels off, when committing matters, when you want validation or pushback. This is the set `/gut` walks (north-star, bitter-lesson, be-water, generative-friction, elder-lens, 50-50, four-wants, fresh-instance, yes-and, energy, target-audience).
- **Read for understanding** — how partnership-thinking *works*; the shapes of the work itself. Loaded when you're building or re-anchoring your mental model (human-as-compound-element, mirror-principle, system-as-harness, centripetal-vs-centrifugal, anti-tribble).

Same parent (a lens, a way of seeing), different reach-moments (apply vs understand). *Who's in the room* (the human, the AI) isn't a lens; that context lives in `Core/you/` and `Core/opus/`.

*(These lists are an enumeration and enumerations drift; `ls Core/lenses/` is the truth. They earn the drift risk because a bare filename list teaches nothing about which job each lens does. If they ever disagree with the folder, believe the folder.)*

---

## How `/gut` walks this

The `/gut` skill walks the decision-lenses against something you're weighing. See `Core/skills/gut/SKILL.md`. Walk all of them, or pick the one that fits. Both are fine.

---

## You can't mess it up

These are starter shapes, not fixed ones. Remove lenses that don't connect. Add ones that do. The architecture invites this; you can't break it by inhabiting it.

---

## Adding a lens

Write the file, give it a question at the top, let it grow. Flat folder, no sub-foldering needed.
